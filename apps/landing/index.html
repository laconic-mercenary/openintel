― what is openintel? ―

Encrypted geolocated notes and photos.

― steps to use ―

1. Log in with a passwordless email code.

2. Set an encryption password — keep it somewhere safe.

3. Enable geolocation and start collecting.

― how it works ―

We do not store your password — it is used to generate an encryption key locally, which locks your notes, photos, and locations before they are sent anywhere. Your notes, photos, and locations reach the server already encrypted.
